Our Story

Who We Are

We've been in business for 7 years and in the electronics market for about 12. In that time we've built our business around one thing: working with other wholesalers and distributors who need quantity, fair pricing, and a supplier who picks up the phone.

We're a strong, motivated team based in Miami, FL. We buy and sell a wide range of brand new, factory sealed electronics, including iPhones, tablets, laptops, headphones, streaming devices, and gaming consoles, along with many other items not listed on this site.
